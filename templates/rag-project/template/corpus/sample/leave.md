# Leave policy

A sample document: replace it with your own.

## Annual leave

Full-time staff receive 20 days of annual leave each year. Part-time staff receive a pro-rata
amount. Leave is booked in the HR portal and approved by your manager.

## Carrying leave over

Up to five unused days of annual leave can be carried over into the next year. Anything above
five days lapses on 31 March.

## Sick leave

You can take up to ten days of paid sick leave a year. A medical certificate from a doctor is
needed for an absence of more than two consecutive days.
