# Expenses

## Submitting a claim

Submit expense claims in the expenses system within 30 days of spending the money, with a receipt
for anything over 20 dollars. Claims older than 90 days are not reimbursed.

## Client entertainment

Client entertainment, such as a client dinner, must be approved by your manager in advance. Alcohol
such as wine or beer is reimbursed only at client dinners, up to two drinks per person.

## What is not reimbursed

Fines, personal travel upgrades, minibar charges and gifts for colleagues are not reimbursed.
