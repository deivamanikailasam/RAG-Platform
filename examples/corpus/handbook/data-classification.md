# Data classification

## The four levels

Public: may be shared with anyone. Internal: for staff only. Confidential: customer data, personal
information and contracts. Restricted: executive, legal and security matters, named people only.

## Handling customer data

Customer data is confidential. It may only be stored in approved company systems and must never be
copied into personal accounts or unapproved tools.

## Labelling

Label documents with their level in the header. When unsure, treat information as confidential.
