# Annual leave policy

This policy applies from 1 February 2026 and replaces the 2021 annual leave policy.

## Entitlement

Full-time staff get 25 days of annual leave per year, plus public holidays. Part-time staff get the
same entitlement in proportion to their contracted hours. Leave accrues from your first day.

## Carrying leave over

You may carry over up to 10 unused days into the next calendar year. Days above 10 are paid out at
your base rate in the January pay run. Unused holidays are never lost without being paid.

## Booking leave

Book annual leave in the HR portal and get your manager's approval before you travel. Give at least
two weeks' notice for absences longer than five working days.
