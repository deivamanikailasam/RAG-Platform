# Travel policy

## Flights

Book flights through the company travel portal. Economy class is the default. Business class is
allowed only for flights longer than eight hours, for example Sydney to London, and needs approval
from your director.

## Hotels

Choose hotels from the travel portal's preferred list, up to 250 dollars a night in capital cities.

## Per diem

The per diem (daily allowance) for meals on a domestic trip is 90 dollars a day. The international
per diem is 150 dollars a day. Receipts are not needed for per diem amounts.
