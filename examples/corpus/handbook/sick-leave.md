# Sick leave

## Entitlement

Staff get 10 days of paid sick leave per year. Unused sick leave carries over from year to year.

## Telling your manager

If you are ill and unable to work, tell your manager before your usual start time, by phone or chat.

## Medical certificates

A doctor's note (medical certificate) is needed only for absences of more than two consecutive days,
or for a sick day next to a public holiday. A pharmacist's certificate is also accepted.

## Caring for others

You may use sick leave as carer's leave to look after an ill member of your household.
