# Code of conduct

## Respect at work

Treat colleagues, customers and suppliers with respect. Harassment and discrimination are never
acceptable and can be reported confidentially to HR.

## Gifts and hospitality

You may accept small gifts worth less than 100 dollars. Gifts or hospitality from a supplier or
customer worth more, such as event or concert tickets, must be declared in the gifts register within
five working days, and may need to be declined.

## Conflicts of interest

Declare any personal interest in a supplier, customer or competitor to your manager.
