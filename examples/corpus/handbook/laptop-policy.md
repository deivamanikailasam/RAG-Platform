# Laptops and devices

## Replacement

Laptops are replaced every three years, or sooner if they fail. Request a replacement through the
IT Service Desk portal.

## Lost or stolen devices

If your laptop or phone is lost or stolen, for example left in a taxi, report it to the IT Service
Desk within one hour so it can be locked and wiped remotely. Report a theft to the police as well.

## Personal use

Light personal use of a company laptop is allowed. Don't install unapproved software.
