# Parental leave policy

## Primary carers

The primary carer of a new child, by birth, adoption or permanent care, receives 18 weeks of
parental leave at full pay, plus superannuation on the unpaid portion of any extended leave.

## Secondary carers

A secondary carer receives 4 weeks of parental leave at full pay, to be taken within 12 months of the
birth or placement.

## Taking leave in blocks

Parental leave can be taken in one continuous period or split into up to two blocks, as long as all
of it is taken within 24 months of the birth or placement. Tell HR at least eight weeks before each
block starts.

## Keeping in touch

You may work up to 10 paid keeping-in-touch days during parental leave without ending it.
