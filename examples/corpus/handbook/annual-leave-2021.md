# Annual leave policy (2021, superseded)

This version applied from March 2021 until January 2026. It has been replaced by the current annual
leave policy and is kept for reference only.

## Entitlement

Full-time staff get 20 days of annual leave per year. Part-time staff get a pro-rata entitlement.

## Carrying leave over

Unused days cannot be carried over into the next year. Any unused leave is forfeited on 31 December.

## Booking leave

Request annual leave by email to your manager at least four weeks before the first day of leave.
