# Remote work

## How many days

Staff may work from home up to three days a week, agreed with their manager. Teams choose at least
two shared office days so people meet in person.

## Home office allowance

New remote workers receive a one-off allowance of 500 dollars for setting up a home office: a desk,
an ergonomic chair or a monitor. Claim it through the expenses system within three months.

## Working abroad

Working from another country needs approval from HR and Legal, for tax and data protection reasons,
and is limited to four weeks a year.
