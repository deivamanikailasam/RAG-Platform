# Using AI tools

## Approved tools

Use the company AI assistant for work. It runs on approved models and keeps your data inside the
company.

## Public AI tools

Never paste customer data, personal information or confidential documents into public AI tools such
as ChatGPT or other consumer chatbots. Public information may be used with care.

## Checking the output

You are responsible for anything you send or publish: check AI output for errors before using it.
