# Security incident response

## Reporting

If you clicked a link in a suspicious or phishing email, or think your account is compromised, tell
the security team at once: use the "Report phishing" button in your mail client or post in the
#security-help channel. Don't delete the email. Reporting quickly matters more than being sure.

## Severity levels

SEV1: customer data exposed or a critical system down. A SEV1 must be acknowledged by the on-call
security engineer within 15 minutes and is reviewed by the CISO.
SEV2: a single system or account affected, acknowledged within 4 hours.

## After an incident

Every SEV1 and SEV2 gets a blameless post-incident review within five working days.
